Key Assumptions to Test

Hypotheses that will be validated within 12 weeks of launch

A1

Automation Drives Consistency

Customers with automation enabled will contribute more consistently than manual-only pot customers.

Validation threshold

Automated cohort is at least 1.5x more likely to contribute in two consecutive months

Method: Compare month-over-month contribution continuity across cohorts

A2

Contextual Entry Points Boost Activation

Entry points tied to salary and card settings will increase activation versus a savings-hub-only entry model.

Validation threshold

Contextual entry points outperform hub-only entry by at least 20% on activation and setup-completion rates

Method: Compare activation and setup-completion rates by entry point

Out of Scope for MMP

Rabbit holes deliberately excluded to maintain focus on the core habit loop

Interest-Bearing Products

Not a new financial product structure. Focus is on habit formation, not product launch.

Shared or Family Pots

Shared ownership introduces permissions and social UX complexity not needed to prove the core loop.

External Bank Funding

Staying inside the {Company XYZ} ecosystem keeps the experience simpler and more trustworthy.

Investment-Linked Goals

Investing serves a different job and belongs in a separate product stream.

Systems & Compliance Dependencies

Critical platform dependencies required for MMP delivery

Current-Account Ledger

Must reflect real available balances and completed transfers accurately. Inconsistency kills trust.

Internal Transfer Service

Must support recurring contributions, round-up transfers, withdrawals, and pause/stop controls.

Card Transaction Event Feed

Round-ups triggered by eligible posted card activity. Unreliable events = failed round-ups.

Refund & Reconciliation Rules

Refunds, reversals, and partial settlements need explicit handling. Exceptions create visible trust problems.

Notification Infrastructure

Must support failure alerts, target-reached moments, and configurable nudges. Core to the MMP feedback loop.

Financial Disclosures & Accessibility

Must explain where funds sit and meet WCAG 2.1 AA. RTL and localization support required.
