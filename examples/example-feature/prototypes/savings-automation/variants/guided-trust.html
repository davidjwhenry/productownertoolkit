Card round-ups
Exactly how round-ups work
  • Spare change moves only from posted card purchases — pending authorisations never trigger a transfer.
  • Refunds reverse the round-up that was moved, so you never lose out on returned purchases.
  • Round-ups transfer once a day, whenever the day's total is at least 1p.
  • Everything stops immediately when you pause automation.
Good to know
  • Round-ups work alongside your recurring contribution, not instead of it.
  • You keep earning nothing extra — pots hold your money, they do not invest it.
