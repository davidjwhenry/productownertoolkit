## Team Context

Use this file for durable team and stakeholder context that helps future drafting, review, and communication.

## Suggested Contents

- Core team and stakeholder roles
- Decision-makers and approval paths
- Working relationships and collaboration patterns
- Durable preferences that affect how work should be prepared or shared

## Rules

- Keep meeting-specific detail in the relevant note instead of here
- Summarise patterns, do not copy verbatim quotes
- Do not store sensitive personal information
